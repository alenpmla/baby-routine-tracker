# Phase 4 addendum — Bottle quantity (amount, ml/oz)

## Goal

Record how much was in the bottle with a unit (ml or oz), matching the solids pattern.

## Requirements

- Tapping **Bottle** opens the feed form asking amount (required) and unit (ml or oz).
- Backfill and edit of a bottle feed ask/keep the quantity.
- Lists and dashboard show e.g. "Bottle · 150 ml".
- Switching to/from Bottle sets or clears the quantity fields.

## Acceptance criteria

- Bottle requires amount (>0) and unit (ml or oz).
- Editing keeps/updates quantity; switching away clears it; switching to Bottle requires it.
- Quantity shows on the Feeding list and Dashboard.
- Build + tests pass.
