# Phase 4 addendum — Data & Reports Settings sub-screen

## Goal

Move the **Data** (export/import) and **Report** (PDF) cards out of the main Settings screen into their own **Data & reports** sub-screen, consistent with the Units and Food suggestions sub-screens.

## Acceptance criteria

- Main Settings shows a "Data & reports" nav row that opens a sub-screen containing the export/import card and the PDF report card.
- The sub-screen has a leading M3 back arrow; back returns to Settings.
- The main Settings screen keeps Appearance + the nav rows (Units, Food suggestions, Data & reports).
- Build + tests pass.
